Cybersecurity Insight: Password Entropy

Entropy is a mathematical measurement of a password's unpredictability, calculated in bits.

  • Formula: Entropy = L × log₂(Pool Size) where L is length.
  • < 35 bits: Extremely vulnerable to local brute-forcing.
  • 60 - 80 bits: Good resistance to online dictionary attacks.
  • 128+ bits: Cryptographically secure. Uncrackable even by supercomputers running for millions of years.

Aura Vault uses window.crypto.getRandomValues, tapping into the OS's entropy pool, making outputs fully random and non-deterministic.
